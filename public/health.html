Everyday medicine cabinet

Keep essentials such as pain relief, plasters, a thermometer, and hand sanitizer within reach. Check expiry dates regularly and store medicines away from children.

Stay well hydrated

Water supports energy, concentration, and recovery. Keep a bottle nearby and increase your intake during hot weather or physical activity.

Small habits matter

Wash your hands often, rest when unwell, and speak with a qualified healthcare professional when symptoms persist or worsen.
